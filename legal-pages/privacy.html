1 Who We Are

Nayalyzer is a social media analytics platform that helps content creators and brands track performance metrics across platforms including TikTok, Instagram, and Threads. Our service connects to social media platforms via their official APIs to retrieve analytics data on your behalf.

2 Data We Collect

  • Email address and name (used to create your Nayalyzer account)
  • Authentication tokens (stored encrypted, never exposed)

3 How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to:

  • Display analytics dashboards and reports within the Service
  • Sync your latest social media metrics automatically
  • Calculate engagement rates, performance scores, and audience insights
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of Nayalyzer
  • Send service-related communications (e.g., sync failures, account alerts)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for third parties, or any purpose beyond providing the analytics service.

4 TikTok Data Specifically

Nayalyzer accesses TikTok data through the TikTok for Developers API under TikTok's Platform Terms of Service. Specifically:

  • We request only the minimum scopes necessary to retrieve your content metrics
  • TikTok data is used exclusively to display analytics within Nayalyzer — it is not shared, sold, or used for any other purpose
  • We do not post, publish, or modify any content on your TikTok account
  • You can revoke Nayalyzer's access to your TikTok account at any time from TikTok's app settings
  • Upon disconnection or account deletion, your TikTok data is removed from our systems within 30 days

5 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Supabase (database hosting) under strict data processing agreements
  • Legal obligations: If required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, with notice to users

6 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase, with servers located in the United States. We implement the following security measures:

  • Encryption of authentication tokens at rest (AES-256-GCM)
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Row-level security policies restricting data access per workspace
  • Regular security reviews

7 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Specifically:

  • Content metrics: Retained for up to 12 months for trend analysis
  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Social media tokens: Deleted immediately upon disconnecting a platform

You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us.

8 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdrawal of consent: Disconnect your social accounts at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact.dinurpradipta@gmail.com.

9 Children's Privacy

Nayalyzer is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
